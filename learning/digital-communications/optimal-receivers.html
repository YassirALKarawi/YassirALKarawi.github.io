01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

The optimum AWGN receiver does not need the entire continuous waveform after observation. Correlation with a finite set of basis functions compresses it into sufficient statistics without losing information relevant to the decision.

INTUITION

A matched filter is a template matcher. It adds waveform portions that agree with the expected symbol coherently while unrelated noise partly cancels. Sampling at the designed instant captures the largest signal-to-noise ratio.

05

Maximum output SNR

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about maximum output snr. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

\left.\frac{S}{N}\right|_{T}=\frac{2E_s}{N_0}
Engineering interpretation

For a real baseband signal under a common two-sided PSD convention.

STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION

Why the matched filter maximizes SNR

At the sampling instant, the output signal is an inner product between H(f) and S(f), while noise power depends on the energy of H(f).

  1. Write the output SNR ratio

    \mathrm{SNR}=\frac{|\int H(f)S(f)e^{j2\pi fT}df|^2}{(N_0/2)\int|H(f)|^2df}

  2. Apply Cauchy–Schwarz

    |\langle H,G\rangle|^2\le\|H\|^2\|G\|^2

  3. Equality requires proportional functions

    H(f)=KS^*(f)e^{-j2\pi fT}

  4. Transform back to time

    h(t)=Ks^*(T-t)
How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.
