01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

Sampling converts a continuous-time waveform into a sequence while preserving its information when the rate is high enough. In frequency, sampling creates shifted copies of the original spectrum. Reconstruction succeeds only when these copies do not overlap.

INTUITION

A camera that photographs a rotating wheel too slowly can make it appear to reverse. Aliasing in communication signals is the same ambiguity: several continuous frequencies produce the same samples.

Sampling sequence

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about sampling sequence. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

x[n]=x(nT_s),\quad T_s=1/f_s
Engineering interpretation

03

Aliased frequency

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about aliased frequency. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

f_a=|f-kf_s|
Engineering interpretation

Choose integer k so that 0≤fₐ≤fₛ/2.

04

Ideal reconstruction

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about ideal reconstruction. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

x(t)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}x[n]\,\mathrm{sinc}\!\left(\frac{t-nT_s}{T_s}\right)
Engineering interpretation

Every sample weights a shifted sinc pulse.

STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION

Why the Nyquist rate is 2B

Sampling multiplies x(t) by an impulse train. Multiplication in time becomes convolution in frequency.

  1. Write the sampling train

    p(t)=\sum_n\delta(t-nT_s)

  2. Transform the product

    X_s(f)=\frac1{T_s}\sum_kX(f-kf_s)

  3. Each replica has width 2B

    |f|\le B

  4. Prevent adjacent replicas from overlapping

    f_s-B>B\;\Rightarrow\;f_s>2B
How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.
