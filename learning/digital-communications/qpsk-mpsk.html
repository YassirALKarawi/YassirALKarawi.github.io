01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

QPSK sends two bits per symbol by selecting one of four phases. It can be interpreted as two orthogonal BPSK streams on in-phase and quadrature carriers. Higher-order PSK increases spectral efficiency but reduces angular separation.

INTUITION

The constellation is a compass. QPSK chooses one of four directions; noise nudges the received point. Gray mapping ensures a small nudge across the nearest boundary usually changes only one bit.

04

Minimum distance

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about minimum distance. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

d_{min}=2\sqrt{E_s}\sin(\pi/M)
Engineering interpretation

Distance shrinks as M grows at fixed Eₛ.

05

Rate

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about rate. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

R_s=R_b/\log_2M
Engineering interpretation

QPSK needs half the symbol rate of BPSK.

STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION

Minimum M-PSK distance

Adjacent PSK points and the origin form an isosceles triangle with radius √Eₛ and included angle 2π/M.

  1. Apply the cosine rule

    d^2=E_s+E_s-2E_s\cos(2\pi/M)

  2. Use 1−cos2x=2sin²x

    d^2=4E_s\sin^2(\pi/M)

  3. Take the positive square root

    d_{min}=2\sqrt{E_s}\sin(\pi/M)

  4. Interpret the limit

    M\uparrow\;\Rightarrow\;d_{min}\downarrow
How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.
