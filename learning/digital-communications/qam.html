01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

QAM varies both in-phase and quadrature amplitudes, packing many points into the signal plane efficiently. It is central to Wi-Fi, LTE, 5G and cable systems, but its amplitude variation demands accurate gain control and a sufficiently linear power amplifier.

INTUITION

PSK places every point on one circle; QAM uses a grid. The grid packs points more efficiently, but corner points are stronger than inner points, producing envelope variation.

02

Mathematical foundation

Equations you can trust

Read every formula as a relationship between physical quantities. Check units before inserting numbers, and keep linear ratios separate from decibels.

01

Complex symbol

s_m=I_m+jQ_m

I and Q take discrete amplitude levels.

02

Bits per symbol

k=\log_2M

16-QAM carries 4 bits; 64-QAM carries 6.

03

Square-QAM energy

E_s=\frac{2}{3}(M-1)d^2

When adjacent levels are separated by 2d.

04

Approximate BER

P_b\approx\frac{4}{\log_2M}\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt M}\right)Q\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{3\log_2M}{M-1}\frac{E_b}{N_0}}\right)

Gray-coded square M-QAM in AWGN.

05

EVM

\mathrm{EVM}_{rms}=\sqrt{\frac{\sum|r_k-s_k|^2}{\sum|s_k|^2}}

Normalized constellation error magnitude.

How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.

06

Deliberate practice

Problems with guided solutions

Attempt each problem on paper before opening the solution. Include units and state the formula you selected.

01Bits per 256-QAM symbol?
Solution

Eight.

02Why must a QAM PA be linear?
Solution

AM/AM and AM/PM distortion move unequal-amplitude points and cause spectral regrowth.

03What does EVM quantify?
Solution

RMS distance between received and ideal constellation points.

04Why Gray-map QAM?
Solution

To limit bit errors when a point crosses one nearest boundary.

05At fixed power, does larger M improve noise margin?
Solution

No; points become closer and require higher SNR.
