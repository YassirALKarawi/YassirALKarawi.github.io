01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

Pulse-code modulation maps each sampled amplitude to one of L permitted levels and encodes the level index as a binary word. Quantization is intentionally lossy: increasing word length lowers distortion but increases bit rate and required bandwidth.

INTUITION

Quantization is like measuring with a ruler whose markings have finite spacing. Values between markings must be rounded; more markings mean a smaller error but more bits are needed to name each mark.

02

Mathematical foundation

Equations you can trust

Read every formula as a relationship between physical quantities. Check units before inserting numbers, and keep linear ratios separate from decibels.

01

Levels

L=2^n

An n-bit word labels 2ⁿ reconstruction levels.

02

Step size

\Delta=\frac{V_{max}-V_{min}}{L}

Uniform quantizer spacing.

03

Noise power

\sigma_q^2\approx\frac{\Delta^2}{12}

Valid for a busy signal and small uniform error.

04

PCM bit rate

R_b=n f_s

Every sample produces n transmitted bits.

05

Sine-wave SQNR

\mathrm{SQNR}_{dB}\approx6.02n+1.76

Ideal full-scale sine and uniform quantization.

How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.

From symbols to numbers

Fully worked examples

Worked example 1

Eight-bit PCM

Problem

A ±1 V range uses 8 bits. Find L and Δ.

  1. L=2⁸=256
  2. Total range=2 V
  3. Δ=2/256=7.8125 mV
AnswerResolution is approximately 7.81 mV.
Worked example 2

Telephone PCM rate

Problem

Speech is sampled at 8 kHz and encoded with 8 bits/sample.

  1. Rᵦ=nfₛ
  2. Rᵦ=8×8000
AnswerRᵦ=64 kbit/s.

Deliberate practice

Problems with guided solutions

Attempt each problem on paper before opening the solution. Include units and state the formula you selected.

01How many levels are available with 10 bits?
Solution

1024 levels.

02Find PCM rate for 12 bits at 44.1 kHz.
Solution

529.2 kbit/s per channel.

03What is ideal SQNR for 8 bits?
Solution

About 49.92 dB.

04Why compand speech?
Solution

It gives small signals finer effective resolution and improves perceived SQNR.

05What happens to Δ when n rises by one bit?
Solution

The number of levels doubles and Δ halves for a fixed range.
