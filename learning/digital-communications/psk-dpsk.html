01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

BPSK uses two antipodal waveforms separated by π radians. This maximizes Euclidean distance for a given bit energy and gives excellent binary error performance in AWGN. Differential encoding trades some performance for simpler phase-reference handling.

INTUITION

BPSK pushes the two hypotheses to opposite ends of a line. Noise must cross the entire midpoint to cause an error, which is why antipodal signalling is stronger than OOK at equal average bit energy.

Mathematical foundation

Equations you can trust

Read every formula as a relationship between physical quantities. Check units before inserting numbers, and keep linear ratios separate from decibels.

01

BPSK

s_b(t)=\sqrt{\frac{2E_b}{T_b}}\cos(2\pi f_ct+\pi b)

b∈{0,1}; phases differ by π.

02

Signal points

s_0=+\sqrt{E_b},\quad s_1=-\sqrt{E_b}

Antipodal points on one basis axis.

03

Coherent BER

P_b=Q\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{2E_b}{N_0}}\right)

Reference benchmark in AWGN.

04

Differential encoding

d_k=b_k\oplus d_{k-1}

Information is carried by phase change.

05

DBPSK BER

P_b=\frac12e^{-E_b/N_0}

For conventional differential detection in AWGN.

How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.

Worked example 1

BPSK BER

Problem

Estimate BER at Eᵦ/N₀=6 dB using Q(√(2γᵦ)).

  1. γᵦ=10^(6/10)=3.981
  2. Argument=√(7.962)=2.822
  3. Q(2.822)≈0.00239
AnswerBER≈2.4×10⁻³.
Worked example 2

Differential sequence

Problem

Differentially encode b=1 0 1 1 with d₀=0.

  1. d₁=1⊕0=1
  2. d₂=0⊕1=1
  3. d₃=1⊕1=0
  4. d₄=1⊕0=1
AnswerEncoded sequence: 1 1 0 1.

01What is the BPSK phase separation?
Solution

π rad or 180°.

02Where is the ideal threshold?
Solution

At zero between ±√Eᵦ.

03What is coherent detection?
Solution

Correlation with a synchronized phase reference.

04Why use differential encoding?
Solution

To resolve absolute phase ambiguity and enable differential detection.

05Which has lower AWGN BER: coherent BPSK or DBPSK?
Solution

Coherent BPSK.
