01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

BPSK uses two antipodal waveforms separated by π radians. This maximizes Euclidean distance for a given bit energy and gives excellent binary error performance in AWGN. Differential encoding trades some performance for simpler phase-reference handling.

INTUITION

BPSK pushes the two hypotheses to opposite ends of a line. Noise must cross the entire midpoint to cause an error, which is why antipodal signalling is stronger than OOK at equal average bit energy.

STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION

Coherent BPSK error probability

The matched-filter output is Gaussian around ±√Eᵦ. An error occurs when noise crosses the zero boundary.

  1. Decision statistic under bit 1

    z=\sqrt{E_b}+n

  2. Matched-filter noise variance

    \sigma_n^2=N_0/2

  3. Normalize the distance to the threshold

    \frac{\sqrt{E_b}}{\sqrt{N_0/2}}=\sqrt{2E_b/N_0}

  4. Use the Gaussian upper tail

    P_b=Q\!\left(\sqrt{2E_b/N_0}\right)
How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.
