COURSE SYLLABUS

Topics and learning sequence

  1. Basic elements of digital communication systems; communication channels.
  2. Sampling and reconstruction; PAM, PWM and PPM.
  3. Quantization, PCM and noise in pulse modulation.
  4. DPCM, delta modulation and adaptive delta modulation.
  5. ASK, PSK, FSK, QPSK and differential PSK.
  6. DEPSK, MSK, M-ary FSK, M-ary PSK, QAM and M-ary QAM.
  7. Optimum receivers for AWGN; receiver for signals corrupted by AWGN.
  8. Performance of memoryless modulation; power, bandwidth, data rate and error probability trade-offs.
  9. Multiplexing: TDM, FDM and applications.

Practical component

Representation of digital signals; pulse modulation; M-PSK, M-FSK and M-QAM modems; TDM and FDM.
