01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

Binary FSK represents the two bits by two carrier frequencies. With a suitable spacing, the tones are orthogonal over the bit interval, so correlators can distinguish them even though both symbols have constant envelope.

INTUITION

Imagine two musical notes. The receiver asks which matched resonator collected more energy. Because amplitude is not the label, FSK tolerates nonlinear power amplification well.

02

Mathematical foundation

Equations you can trust

Read every formula as a relationship between physical quantities. Check units before inserting numbers, and keep linear ratios separate from decibels.

01

BFSK signals

s_i(t)=A\cos(2\pi f_i t+\phi),\quad i\in\{0,1\}

Each bit selects a tone.

02

Orthogonality

\int_0^{T_b}s_0(t)s_1(t)dt=0

Zero cross-correlation over the bit interval.

03

Minimum coherent spacing

\Delta f=|f_1-f_0|=\frac{1}{2T_b}

One common orthogonal spacing convention.

04

Carson-style estimate

B\approx2(\Delta f+R_b)

Useful engineering estimate for binary FSK.

05

Coherent BFSK BER

P_b=Q\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{E_b}{N_0}}\right)

Orthogonal coherent binary signalling.

How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.

From symbols to numbers

Fully worked examples

Worked example 1

Orthogonal spacing

Problem

Find the minimum coherent tone spacing for Rᵦ=20 kbit/s.

  1. Tᵦ=1/20000=50 μs
  2. Δf=1/(2Tᵦ)
AnswerΔf=10 kHz.
Worked example 2

Bandwidth estimate

Problem

Use Δf=10 kHz and Rᵦ=20 kbit/s.

  1. B≈2(Δf+Rᵦ)
  2. B≈2(10+20) kHz
AnswerApproximately 60 kHz.

Deliberate practice

Problems with guided solutions

Attempt each problem on paper before opening the solution. Include units and state the formula you selected.

01Find Tᵦ at 100 kbit/s.
Solution

10 μs.

02Find minimum coherent Δf at 100 kbit/s.
Solution

50 kHz.

03Why is FSK constant-envelope?
Solution

The selected sinusoid keeps nearly constant amplitude.

04How does a noncoherent receiver decide?
Solution

It compares detected tone energies without needing carrier phase.

05What hardware can generate continuous-phase FSK?
Solution

A voltage-controlled oscillator or phase-continuous synthesizer.
