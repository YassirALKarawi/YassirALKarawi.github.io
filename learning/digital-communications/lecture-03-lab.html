Quantization and PCM

Round samples to permitted levels, inspect binary words, and connect bit depth to error, bit rate and SQNR.

Measure, round, encode

PCM sends one binary word for each quantized sample. With n bits per sample there are L = 2n labels.

Δ = 2Vmax/L   ·   Rb = nfs

The laboratory uses a uniform mid-rise quantizer over −1 to +1 V. Each cell is represented by its midpoint. Inputs outside the range are clipped. The error is e = x − Q(x).

σq2 ≈ Δ2/12   ·   SQNR ≈ 6.02n + 1.76 dB

The noise-power formula assumes approximately uniform error and no overload. The SQNR expression is the full-scale sinusoid approximation. The measured error displayed below is calculated directly and can differ.

Companding compresses amplitude before quantization and expands it after decoding. It gives more effective resolution to small signals. The μ-law curve is F(x) = sign(x) ln(1 + μ|x|)/ln(1 + μ), for normalized input.

Worked practice

Find the step and bit rate for 3-bit PCM over −1 to +1 V at 8 ksample/s.

Levels: L = 2³ = 8.

Step: Δ = 2/8 = 0.25 V.

Bit rate: Rb = 3 × 8000 = 24 kbit/s.

What happens when one bit is added?

The number of levels doubles.

At fixed input range the step halves.

Under the uniform-error model the noise power becomes one quarter, giving about 6 dB improvement.
