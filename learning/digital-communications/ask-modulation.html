01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

Amplitude shift keying conveys data by selecting carrier amplitudes. On-off keying is the binary special case in which a one transmits a carrier burst and a zero transmits no carrier. Simplicity is gained at the cost of sensitivity to amplitude fading and interference.

INTUITION

ASK is a lamp controlled by bits: on for one, off for zero. The detector does not inspect one instantaneous sample; it collects evidence over the whole bit interval and compares the result with a threshold.

02

Mathematical foundation

Equations you can trust

Read every formula as a relationship between physical quantities. Check units before inserting numbers, and keep linear ratios separate from decibels.

01

OOK waveforms

s_1(t)=A\cos(2\pi f_ct),\quad s_0(t)=0

Defined over 0≤t<Tᵦ.

02

Energy of one

E_1=\int_0^{T_b}s_1^2(t)dt=\frac{A^2T_b}{2}

Assumes an integer number of carrier cycles.

03

Average power

P_{avg}=P(1)\frac{A^2}{2}

For equiprobable bits this becomes A²/4.

04

Coherent OOK BER

P_b=Q\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{E_b}{N_0}}\right)

Here Eᵦ denotes average energy per information bit for equiprobable OOK.

05

Rectangular-pulse bandwidth

B_{NN}\approx2R_b

Approximate passband null-to-null width.

How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.

From symbols to numbers

Fully worked examples

Worked example 1

Energy and power

Problem

A=2 V, Tᵦ=1 μs and P(1)=0.5. Find E₁ and average power for 1 Ω.

  1. E₁=A²Tᵦ/2=2 μJ
  2. Eavg=E₁/2=1 μJ
  3. Pavg=Eavg/Tᵦ
AnswerPavg=1 W.
Worked example 2

Bandwidth

Problem

Estimate null-to-null bandwidth at Rᵦ=100 kbit/s.

  1. B≈2Rᵦ
  2. B≈2×100 kHz
AnswerApproximately 200 kHz.
06

Deliberate practice

Problems with guided solutions

Attempt each problem on paper before opening the solution. Include units and state the formula you selected.

01Find E₁ for A=4 V and Tᵦ=2 μs across 1 Ω.
Solution

E₁=16 μJ.

02Find average power for equiprobable OOK with A=4 V.
Solution

A²/4=4 W for 1 Ω.

03Why is coherent detection phase-sensitive?
Solution

The local carrier must align with the received carrier to accumulate the signal correctly.

04What happens to BER when Eᵦ/N₀ rises?
Solution

It decreases monotonically.

05Why is ASK vulnerable to fading?
Solution

Fading directly changes the amplitude used to distinguish the symbols.
