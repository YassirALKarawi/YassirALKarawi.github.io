01

Start with meaning

Why this topic matters

Amplitude shift keying conveys data by selecting carrier amplitudes. On-off keying is the binary special case in which a one transmits a carrier burst and a zero transmits no carrier. Simplicity is gained at the cost of sensitivity to amplitude fading and interference.

INTUITION

ASK is a lamp controlled by bits: on for one, off for zero. The detector does not inspect one instantaneous sample; it collects evidence over the whole bit interval and compares the result with a threshold.

02

Energy of one

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about energy of one. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

E_1=\int_0^{T_b}s_1^2(t)dt=\frac{A^2T_b}{2}
Engineering interpretation

Assumes an integer number of carrier cycles.

03

Average power

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about average power. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

P_{avg}=P(1)\frac{A^2}{2}
Engineering interpretation

For equiprobable bits this becomes A²/4.

Coherent OOK BER

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about coherent ook ber. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

P_b=Q\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{E_b}{N_0}}\right)
Engineering interpretation

Here Eᵦ denotes average energy per information bit for equiprobable OOK.

05

Rectangular-pulse bandwidth

Before the equation Use this relation when the problem asks about rectangular-pulse bandwidth. First list the known quantities, convert them to SI units, and verify the stated assumptions.

B_{NN}\approx2R_b
Engineering interpretation

Approximate passband null-to-null width.

STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION

Energy of an OOK mark

Energy is the time integral of instantaneous power. For a 1 Ω normalization, power is s₁²(t).

  1. Insert the transmitted mark

    E_1=\int_0^{T_b}A^2\cos^2(2\pi f_ct)dt

  2. Use the identity

    \cos^2x=\frac12(1+\cos2x)

  3. An integer number of cycles makes the cosine term integrate to zero

    \int_0^{T_b}\cos(4\pi f_ct)dt=0

  4. The remaining constant term gives

    E_1=\frac{A^2T_b}{2}
How to study the mathematics

First name every symbol, then state the assumptions, substitute values with units, and only then calculate. A numerically correct result without assumptions is not a complete engineering answer.

01Why is coherent detection phase-sensitive?
Guided solution

The local carrier must align with the received carrier to accumulate the signal correctly.

02What happens to BER when Eᵦ/N₀ rises?
Guided solution

It decreases monotonically.

03Why is ASK vulnerable to fading?
Guided solution

Fading directly changes the amplitude used to distinguish the symbols.

Guided solution

Defined over 0≤t<Tᵦ.
