The central idea

In binary ASK, the amplitude of a changes according to the transmitted bit. A common form, called , sends the carrier for bit 1 and suppresses it for bit 0.

Engineering meaning

ASK is simple to generate, but amplitude disturbances and noise can make its receiver less robust than phase-based modulation.

02

Signal model

s(t) = A m(t) cos(2πfct + φ)
A
Carrier amplitude
m(t)
Binary message, equal to 0 or 1
fc
in hertz
φ
Initial phase in radians

A sufficiently high carrier frequency gives multiple carrier cycles within each bit interval. This makes the amplitude state observable and supports practical filtering and demodulation.

Worked example

Given: An OOK transmitter uses A = 4 V across a normalized 1 Ω load. The bit duration is Tb = 1 ms. Find the energy of a transmitted 1, assuming an integer number of carrier cycles.

  1. E1 = ∫0Tb s²(t) dt
  2. E1 = A² ∫0Tb cos²(2πfct) dt
  3. ∫ cos²(·)dt = Tb/2, therefore E1 = A²Tb/2
  4. E1 = (4² × 0.001)/2 = 0.008 J = 8 mJ
