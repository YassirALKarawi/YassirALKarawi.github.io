The learning loop

Read → manipulate → calculate → prove

01

Build intuition

Plain English explains what changes physically before notation is introduced.

02

Read the equation

Every formula states assumptions, units and the design meaning of each relationship.

03

Touch the signal

Change values and watch waveforms, samples, spectra, constellations and decisions respond.

04

Earn mastery

Worked examples lead into guided problems and a scored quiz with explanatory feedback.
