Research context

Plain-language summary

A cyclic-prefix shifting algorithm for reducing the peak-to-average power ratio in orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing systems.

Research overview

The paper addresses peak-to-average power ratio in OFDM while also considering computational complexity and bit-error performance. It positions the proposed shifting method against amplitude clipping and selected mapping, which have different implementation trade-offs.

Approach and contribution

The abstract describes cyclic shifting of frequency-domain OFDM samples to form alternative arrangements with reduced peak-to-average power ratio. Simulation is used to examine PAPR and bit-error behaviour, with selected mapping as a complexity comparator.

Reported findings

The paper reports PAPR reduction without bit-error-rate degradation in its simulations, and approximately 68% lower computational complexity than the compared selected-mapping scheme.

Evidence and limitations

The 68% figure concerns the stated computational-complexity comparison, not a measured 68% reduction in device energy or battery use.
