# Discovery checks

Use a new conversation for each prompt, with web search enabled. Record the actual app/model, date, exact prompt, answer and clickable source URLs. Do not supply the author, title or website in the topic-discovery test. An exact-title test and a supplied-URL test measure different things.

## Fixed topic prompts

1. Search the web for research papers on adaptive quantum radar target detection in thermal noise. Give verifiable titles, authors, DOIs and source links, and distinguish simulations from experiments.
2. Search the web for research on energy optimisation in solar-powered off-grid Open RAN with reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and edge computing. Give methods, evaluation conditions and original sources.
3. Search the web for research on digital twins and reinforcement learning for cybersecurity in Open RAN. Distinguish quantum-state simulation from classical network deployments and provide original sources.

## Record separately

- Paper discovered through publisher or institutional repository.
- Author website discovered without providing its URL.
- Paper selected as a cited source in the generated answer.
- Title, authors and DOI correctly matched.
- Claimed result actually supported by the linked source.
- Newly published topic guide retrieved, rather than an older cached record.

Never count a supplied-URL reading test as spontaneous discovery. A successful result in one app does not verify another app. A single non-appearance is not evidence of exclusion. Do not report a change in ranking or citations without comparable observations. The new guides are editorial pages, not additional published papers.

## Baseline and update

The 2026-09-08 conversation included search retrieval of the off-grid and cybersecurity paper pages. Gemini identified the radar paper after an exact-title/DOI prompt but initially linked a different paper; the user subsequently supplied the correct URL and Gemini quoted the site's research overview. This is evidence of prompted retrieval, not an unbiased ranking or discovery success.

The topic guides and six CC BY 4.0 original abstracts were prepared after that baseline. There is no measured post-update discovery improvement recorded here. Repeat the same prompts independently before drawing a conclusion.
