Draft policy framework

Standards that protect the record.

These proposed policies will be finalized and versioned before the journal accepts submissions.

Authorship & contributions

Authorship should reflect substantive contribution and accountability. All authors must approve the submitted version. Explain changes to the author list and obtain agreement from affected contributors.

Originality & research integrity

Submit original work and disclose related manuscripts, preprints and overlapping material. Plagiarism, fabricated data, manipulated evidence and undisclosed duplicate publication are unacceptable. Concerns should be investigated fairly with an opportunity to respond.

Competing interests & funding

Declare financial and nonfinancial interests and describe sponsor involvement. Editorial conflicts require reassignment to an independent editor. Fee status must not determine scientific decisions.

Data, code & reproducibility

Include an availability statement with repository links and access conditions where possible. Explain justified restrictions, including privacy, contractual or security constraints. Do not claim unrestricted access where it is not provided.

AI-assisted work

Authors remain accountable for accuracy, originality and references. Disclose substantive AI assistance and verify all outputs. AI systems cannot take authorship responsibility. Confidential manuscripts should not be shared with unauthorized third-party services.

Corrections, expressions of concern & retractions

Maintain a transparent publication record. Material post-publication errors may require a linked correction, an expression of concern or retraction following assessment. Preserve notices and explain the reason for the action.

Complaints & appeals

Provide the article or decision involved, supporting evidence and the remedy sought. Complaints involving an editor should be reviewed independently. A formal contact channel must be activated before submissions open.

Research involving people, animals or sensitive data

Report applicable ethics approvals, consent and data protection measures. Editors may request evidence. Authors must comply with requirements applicable to their research and institutions.

This framework does not represent membership in or endorsement by any publishing standards organization.
