Proposed review framework

Independent judgment.
Explicit criteria.

A transparent assessment process designed to evaluate significance, validity and clarity.

01

Editorial screening

Scope, completeness, originality concerns, declarations and readiness for expert evaluation.

02

External assessment

At least two independent expert reports under the proposed internal policy. Reviewer conflicts must be disclosed.

03

Reasoned decision

Acceptance, revision or rejection based on evidence. Revisions should address each substantive comment.

Reviewer guidance

Assess the research question, novelty, methodological validity, evidence, reproducibility, limitations and presentation. Separate essential corrections from optional suggestions. Provide specific, respectful comments supported by the manuscript.

Confidentiality and responsible tool use

Keep manuscripts and reviews confidential. Do not upload unpublished material into external AI services without authorization. Disclose any conflict or inability to evaluate the work competently.

Review model and timelines

The final anonymity model and service targets will be published before submissions open. No acceptance or review completion time is guaranteed.

Appeals

Authors may request a reasoned reconsideration supported by specific evidence. A conflict-free editor should assess the appeal. A disagreement alone does not establish an error.
