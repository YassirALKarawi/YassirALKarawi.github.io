Privacy & terms

Know what this site does.

A concise notice for the current founding website and its local preparation tool.

Local preparation data

The preparation tool stores your manuscript title, article type and abstract in browser local storage when available. This application does not send these entries to the journal. Use “Clear local draft” to remove them, or clear the site data in your browser.

Hosting and access

Hosting and authentication providers may process technical access information under their own terms. This website does not provide a journal account system or a payment processor.

Cookies & analytics

No advertising trackers or analytics scripts have been added to this application. Browser local storage is used only by the preparation tool. Hosting-level access controls may use separate authentication mechanisms.

Website terms

The content describes a founding project and proposed policies. It does not constitute an offer of publication, an editorial appointment or a claim of registration or indexing. Do not submit confidential research by an unverified channel.

Future operational notice

A complete publisher identity, contact address, applicable privacy information and retention procedures must be published before the journal collects submission or payment data.
