About JECDE

A shared forum for engineering and digital research.

A proposed English-language, open-access journal spanning electrical, electronic, computing and digital disciplines.

Aims & scope

The journal welcomes original theoretical, methodological and experimental contributions across its seven subject tracks. Interdisciplinary work is encouraged when its contribution and evaluation are clearly established.

  • Electrical Engineering: Power systems, renewable energy, electrical machines, control and automation.
  • Electronics: Electronic circuits, semiconductor devices, instrumentation and embedded systems.
  • Computing: Computer architecture, software engineering, algorithms and distributed computing.
  • Communications: Wireless and optical communications, signal processing and communication networks.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Machine learning, intelligent systems, robotics and trustworthy AI.
  • Cybersecurity: Network and system security, cryptography, privacy and digital forensics.
  • Digital Technologies: Internet of Things, digital twins, cloud and edge computing, and digital infrastructure.

Outside the scope

Work outside the listed disciplines; unsupported performance claims; descriptive implementations without substantive research evaluation; and manuscripts without a clear scientific or engineering contribution.

Article types

Original research and critical reviews. Reviews should explain literature selection and provide synthesis beyond a catalogue of publications.
