Start writing in three steps.

  1. Download and extract the ZIP package, or upload its contents to a new Overleaf project.
  2. Select main.tex as the main document and use pdfLaTeX. Keep jecde.cls in the same folder.
  3. Replace the instructional fields, write the manuscript and compile twice to resolve cross-references.
Anonymous review option

Use \documentclass[review]{jecde} to hide the title-block author metadata. This does not anonymize acknowledgments, content or attached files. Inspect the manuscript manually. The journal's final review policy remains pending.
